Last updated: 2026-01-27

Hereits reserves the right to approve or reject vendor registrations
2. Vendor Responsibilities
Maintain accurate business information, pricing, and availability
Provide genuine services and products to customers
Honor confirmed appointments and customer inquiries
Comply with applicable local, state, and national laws
3. Appointments & Customer Interaction
Vendors are responsible for managing appointments and queues
Customer communication may occur via WhatsApp or other supported channels
Hereits is not responsible for service quality or customer disputes
4. Products & Services Listings
All listings must be accurate, lawful, and not misleading
Images and descriptions must belong to the vendor or be legally usable
Prohibited, illegal, or restricted items must not be listed
Hereits reserves the right to remove any listing that violates platform policies.

5. Payments, Fees & Charges
Vendors agree to pay any applicable platform fees as described in their chosen plan.
