Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) for Hereits:

General FAQ

Q1. What is Hereits?
A business management platform to manage appointments, list products/services, and connect with customers online.
Q2. Who can use Hereits?
Small and medium businesses: salons, clinics, service providers, consultants, and local stores.
Q3. Do I need technical knowledge to use Hereits?
No, Hereits is built to be simple and easy to use.
Q4. Is Hereits free to use?
Hereits offers affordable, usage-based pricing. Some features require a paid subscription.

Business FAQ

Q5. How do I register my business on Hereits?
You can sign up using your email address or Google Sign-In, then add your business details to complete registration.
Q6. Can I manage multiple businesses with one account?
Yes, depending on your plan.
Q7. Can I add multiple experts or staff members?
Yes, based on your subscription plan.
Q8. Is my business information visible to customers?
Only the information you publish is visible to customers.

Appointment FAQ

Q9. How does appointment booking work?
Customers book available slots, and the system automatically manages confirmations, queues, and notifications.
Q10. Can I manage appointment queues?
Yes, Hereits offers smart queue management for high-demand periods.
Q11. What happens if a customer does not show up?
You can mark the appointment as a no-show, and the system can advance the queue accordingly.
Q12. Can experts manage appointments themselves?
Yes, experts can manage their own appointments through their individual panel.
Q13. Can customers cancel or reschedule their own appointments?
Yes, customers can cancel or reschedule directly through their booking confirmation, subject to your business's cancellation window.
Q14. Can I set a cancellation policy for my business?
Yes, you can define how close to the appointment time cancellations are allowed.

Services FAQ

Q15. Can I list multiple services on Hereits?
Yes, along with pricing and descriptions for each.
Q16. Can I temporarily disable a service?
Yes, you can do this anytime from your dashboard.
Q17. Can I update service prices?
Yes, you can update prices anytime.

Product FAQ

Q18. Can I list my products on Hereits?
Yes, with images, prices, and descriptions.
Q19. Is there a limit on product listings?
This depends on your plan; some plans include free listings.
Q20. Can customers order products directly?
This depends on the features enabled for your business.
Q21. Can I edit or remove products later?
Yes, you can edit or remove products anytime.

Pricing & Plans FAQ

Q22. What plans does Hereits offer?
Hereits offers flexible plans based on your business size and needs, from a free starter tier to premium plans with advanced features like multi-staff management and unlimited listings. Visit our Pricing page for full details.
Q23. What's included in the free plan vs paid plans?
The free plan lets you list your business and basic services. Paid plans unlock features like multiple staff/expert accounts, unlimited product listings, and appointment queue management.
Q24. Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan anytime?
Yes, you can change your plan anytime from your account settings. Changes take effect from your next billing cycle.

Payments & Billing FAQ

Q25. What payment methods does Hereits accept?
Hereits accepts major debit/credit cards, UPI, and net banking for subscription payments.
Q26. How does billing work?
Subscriptions are billed automatically based on your chosen cycle (monthly or annual). You'll receive a notification before each renewal.
Q27. What is Hereits' refund policy?
Refunds are handled as per our Refund Policy. [Link to Refund Policy page]

Notifications & Communication FAQ
Q28. How will I be notified about new bookings?
You'll receive instant email notifications for new appointments, cancellations, and customer messages.
Q29. Does Hereits send appointment reminders to customers?
Yes, customers receive email reminders before their scheduled appointment to reduce no-shows.

For Customers FAQ
Q30. Is Hereits free for customers to use?
Yes, browsing businesses and booking appointments on Hereits is completely free for customers.
Q31. How do I find businesses near me on Hereits?
Simply search by category or location, and Hereits will show relevant businesses in your area.
Q32. Do I need an account to book an appointment?
A basic account (phone number verification) is required to confirm and manage your bookings.
